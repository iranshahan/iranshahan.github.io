The story

Fifty-three years, and the names that remain

1925
–19791304 – 1357 in the Iranian calendar

Between 1925 and 1979, Iran lived through fifty-three years of building. Railways crossed the Zagros and the Alborz. The University of Tehran opened its doors. Roads, dams, schools and factories rose across the country, and Iranian women won the right to vote. A nation that had been held back for generations began to picture its own future.

Decades later, a new generation went into the streets asking for that future back. In January 2026 the protests that had spread across the country were met with gunfire. Thousands were killed; human rights groups have confirmed thousands of names, and other estimates run far higher. Many were young. Many were never written on any official list.

Iranians call them javidnam, the eternal names. Iran Shahan was made so that both are remembered together: the years in which Iran was built, and the people who died believing it could be free again.

Questions

Before you buy

Is this an investment?

No. Iran Shahan is a commemorative souvenir. Nobody promises anything about its future price, and you should only buy it because you want to keep it.

Where does the money go?

Half goes straight from your wallet to the foundation, and half to the creator, in the same transaction that sends you the souvenirs. The foundation's address is fixed in the contract and can never be changed; you can see it below the purchase steps and check every payment on Tronscan.

What do the numbers mean?

Each souvenir sold takes the next number in line, starting at #101. Numbers #1 to #100 belong to the creator's share. Your numbers are written into the blockchain record of your purchase. EP is a regular TRON token, so the number belongs to the purchase record, not to a particular unit in your wallet.

Why does my wallet ask twice?

The first request lets the souvenir contract take exactly the USDT for this purchase. The second is the purchase. If you buy again later for the same total or less, you may only see the second request.

What are the network fees?

TRON charges a fee in TRX for every transaction. It goes to the network, not to the creator. One purchase costs the same fee whether you buy 1 souvenir or 50, so buying several at once is cheaper per souvenir.

Can the price change?

The creator can raise the price but never set it below 10 USDT. If the price changes between opening this page and confirming in your wallet, the purchase stops and nothing is charged.

Who cannot buy?

Residents of countries whose laws restrict this purchase, including the United States.
